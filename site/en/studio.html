02 / The Studio

Production, teaching, performance:
three roles, one love.

Since 2008, Pixel Studio has moved between the recording studio, the classroom and the stage. Production keeps the teaching close to real music, and the stage gives every lesson something to perform toward.

01 / ProductionMusic productionSongwriting, arranging and recording, including the school song for Chih Lee Institute of Technology and the music for the Ren-An Foundation's charity radio broadcasts.
02 / EducationMusic teachingExperience teaching guitar, piano and singing at twenty school clubs and music schools, gathered into four interactive Labs.
03 / PerformancePerformanceCafé residencies, licensed street performer in six counties and cities, weddings and charity concerts: still on stage, year after year.
03 / The Teacher

Leo LeeLeo | music creation and teaching

From school clubs and music schools to the stage, Leo has kept teaching singing, guitar, keyboard and songwriting, helping students from all kinds of backgrounds turn the music they love, step by step, into their own expression.

School club teaching
  • Guitar club instructor at Oriental Institute of Technology, Huafan University, National Taiwan Ocean University and Chih Lee Institute of Technology.
  • Guitar club instructor at National Taishan Senior High School, New Taipei Municipal Haishan High School, Mingde High School and Qingshui High School.
  • Guitar club instructor at New Taipei Municipal Shiding High School and National Overseas Chinese Experimental High School.
  • Keyboard and lead vocal instructor for the Chinese Culture University popular music club.
Music experience
  • Contracted songwriter with the Campus Golden Melody record production centre.
  • Songwriting lecturer, Department of Digital Multimedia, Asia Pacific Institute of Creativity.
  • Resident singer at Golden Love River Café, Kaohsiung.
  • Taught guitar, pop piano and pop singing at Baroque Music Life in Shulin.
  • Taught guitar, pop piano and pop singing at Big Matt Music World in Banqiao and Canon Arts Academy.
  • Taught guitar at Qin Yi Music Studio in Shulin.
  • Taught guitar and pop piano at Doremi Music School in Xinzhuang.
  • Taught guitar at Ji Le Music Life in Taishan.
  • Taught guitar at KHS music school in Xiaogang, Kaohsiung, and Chun Qin Music School.
  • Taught pop piano at Qin Yun Music School, Kaohsiung.
Club leadership
  • National Kaohsiung Hospitality College guitar club: 10th president, 7th club adviser and 6th head instructor.

  • Taipei Municipal Nangang Vocational High School guitar club, 8th cohort.
Other experience

About Pixel Studio

We Perform the Pixel of Music.

Pixel Studio was founded in 2008 and brings together music production, music teaching and performance.

We explore the many sides of music, look for the tiny "pixels" inside it, then organise them into a system and share it with more people. It's what we do best and love most.
