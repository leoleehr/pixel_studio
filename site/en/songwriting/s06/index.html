The chorus line 「我最親愛的」 is the title and the hook; the melody pushes up from the middle range, and each section ends with a long note and a breath. The foreign composer's melody had no Chinese tones in mind, but Lin Xi set words so the characters on strong beats follow the melody's direction, and it sounds entirely natural.

decides eighty per cent of how much a song matters.

However well the melody is written, if the lead singer is uncomfortable, listeners only hear the strain. Next, find the place that suits your singer best.

Turn01:30–02:30 Full range and usable range

Choose the key by the usable range

Full range

From the lowest to the highest sound you can make, including falsetto and notes you barely reach. For reference only.

Usable range

The range where the tone is steady, you can sing it again and again, and the throat stays relaxed. A song's range should sit within it.

Pitch points

The few notes where the lead singer is most comfortable and resonance is fullest. Put the chorus's long notes and hook on a pitch point or a little higher for the most impact.

Fewer register breaks

With correct vocal technique, the switch between chest and head voice gets smoother. Keep long notes off the register break in the melody to reduce the gap between registers.

TurnChoosing a key

The lead singer's highest usable note decides the song's highest note

Half steps to transpose = highest usable note − song's highest note. Make them the same, or take the song down another one or two half steps to leave some headroom.

What a key change up does

The chorus after the post-chorus often goes up a key to build the atmosphere step by step. Up a half or whole step is most common, and listeners feel the song suddenly brighten.

What a key change up costs

After raising the key, the highest note still has to sit within the lead singer's usable range. When choosing a key, work out the highest note after the change first, then work back to the original key.

Which should you go by when choosing songs and keys?

  1. Full range
  2. Usable range
  3. The original singer's key
  4. The male and female keys on the song sheet
Go by the usable range. Notes you can only just reach will tire you out over a whole song.

The song is written in C and its highest note is A4; the lead singer's highest usable note is F4. Which key should it change to?

  1. A♭
  2. E
  3. F
  4. D
F4 − A4 = −4 half steps, and C down 4 half steps is A♭.
