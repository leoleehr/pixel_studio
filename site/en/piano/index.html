01 / The Learning Journey

Lesson by lesson,
playing your own songs.

The first eight lessons lay the groundwork in reading, fingering, chords and accompaniment, with a play-and-sing mid-course performance in Lesson 8; the last four go deeper into scale degrees, progressions and rhythm styles, with the final performance in Lesson 12. Every lesson is two hours.

About Pixel Studio

We Perform the Pixel of Music.

Pixel Studio was founded in 2008 and brings together music production, music teaching and performance.

Lots of people learned piano as kids and then let it go. We start from chords and accompaniment, with years of piano class materials organised into twelve lessons: first you play and sing, then step by step you move toward free accompaniment and basic arranging in pop piano.
