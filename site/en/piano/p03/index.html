OpenWhat is a scale?

Starting from the tonic, laid out at fixed distances

Scale

Notes laid out from low to high in a fixed order, starting from one note. Major scales are bright and light; minor scales are sad and inward.

Key

Set by the tonic. Any of the twelve half steps of equal temperament can be the tonic, so there are twelve major keys and twelve minor keys.

The C major and A natural minor scales have no sharps or flats, so only white keys are used in those keys. They're relative major and minor, the best starting point for beginners; guitar, piano and singing all start from these two keys.

Two groups of notes

Do Re Mi Fa and Sol La Si Do are each "whole, whole, half", joined by a whole step in the middle. Say it: whole-whole-half, whole, whole-whole-half.

Changing the tonic

Start on G and follow the formula, and the seventh note has to be raised a half step to F♯ to fit the "whole, half" ending. That's why G major has one sharp.

BuildFive fingers, eight notes

When you run out of fingers, the thumb goes under and the middle finger goes over

Thumb-under

Right hand going up: after E (finger 3), the thumb passes under the palm to play F and the hand shifts along. Same for the left hand coming down.

Right hand: 1 2 3 ‧ 1 2 3 4 5

Middle-finger crossing

Right hand coming down: after the thumb plays F, finger 3 crosses over the thumb to play E. Going up, the left hand's finger 3 crosses over the thumb.

Left hand: 5 4 3 2 1 ‧ 3 2 1

Keep the wrist level as the thumb passes under, without twisting outward; move the thumb to its next spot early so the sound stays connected. Start slow so every crossing is quiet and smooth.

  1. D
  2. E

  4. G
1 2 3 play C D E, the thumb passes under to F, then 2 3 4 5 play G A B C.

In a major scale, which two pairs of neighbouring notes are a half step apart?

  1. 1–2、5–6
  2. 3–4、7–1
  3. 2–3、6–7
  4. 4–5、1–2
Mi–Fa and Si–Do are a half step apart; every other neighbouring pair is a whole step.

Practise in sections

Practise the right hand, then the left, and finally put them together. Hands together, the right hand passes under at F and the left crosses over at A, so the two moves are staggered. Start at 50 BPM and add 4 BPM each time it's steady.
