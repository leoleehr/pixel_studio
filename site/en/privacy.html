Privacy policy

Applies to the Pixel Studio Vocal Lab, Guitar Lab, Piano Lab and Songwriting Lab course sites. Last updated: 30 October 2026

The course sites are run by Leo Lee of Pixel Studio ("the teacher"). The sites collect student data for one reason only: so the teacher can follow each student's progress and give feedback in class. This page explains what the sites collect, how it's used and what rights students have.

1. What we collect

Google accountWhen a student signs in with Google, the site receives only the email address and the name on the Google account, to identify the student. The site never reads the student's email, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
Student profileThe name, school department or company, and course code that the student fills in.
Surveys and class activitiesPre-class surveys, course feedback surveys, exit tickets and practice logs, plus results from activities in the slides such as quizzes, rhythm challenges and lyric exercises.
RecordingSinging or playing recordings that the student chooses to upload. The site never records without the student taking action.
Browsing statisticsGoogle Analytics records which pages were viewed, which slide was reached, which class tools were used and how many times surveys were sent or recordings uploaded, together with the device type and rough location that the browser provides automatically. The statistics don't include names, Gmail addresses, answers or recordings.
Device codeA random code generated by the browser to tell apart one student's different devices. It contains no personal information.

2. How the data is used

  • To confirm that the student sending data belongs to the class, so no one else can send data under their name.
  • So the teacher can read surveys before class, organise learning records after class and give feedback on recordings.
  • To measure how the course is going and adjust materials and teaching.

The data is never used for advertising and never sold. Students' names, Gmail addresses, answers and recordings go only to the teacher's Google Sheet and Google Drive and are not shared with any other third party. Google Analytics receives only the browsing statistics listed above.

3. Where the data is kept

  • The teacher's Google Sheet and Google Drive: data and recordings sent by students are kept in the teacher's own Google account. Only the teacher can open them, and recordings are never shared publicly.
  • The student's own browser: sign-in status, student details, and records held temporarily when the connection drops or the teacher pauses data collection are kept in the browser's local storage. On a shared computer, press "Switch user" in the "Student profile" window to clear them.
  • Google Analytics: browsing statistics are kept in the teacher's Google Analytics property for up to 14 months. Google signals and ad personalisation are turned off, so the statistics are never used to serve ads. Google Analytics stores cookies in the browser (names starting with _ga) to tell different visitors apart.

The site itself is hosted on Cloudflare, which only serves the pages and does not store student data.

4. Students' rights

Students can email at any time to ask to see, correct or delete their data, and the teacher will deal with it as soon as possible. Students can also remove Pixel Studio's sign-in connection on the "Third-party apps and services" page of their Google account.

If you'd rather not be counted in the browsing statistics, press the button below. The setting applies only to this browser; you'll need to set it again on another device or after clearing site data.
