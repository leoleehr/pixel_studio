About Pixel Studio

We Perform the Pixel of Music.

Pixel Studio was founded in 2008 and brings together music production, music teaching and performance.

Starting from the 2013 guitar club handbook, we've turned more than ten years of club teaching into twelve chapters, so anyone holding a guitar for the first time can play their own songs, one step at a time.
