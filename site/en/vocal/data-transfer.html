Local Record Transfer

Bring your practice records from the old site

The old site and Pixel Studio live on different domains, so your browser won't move your local practice records over by itself. First download a backup on the device and browser you used before, then come back here to import it.

  1. Open the old site's record export page and download the JSON file.
  2. Come back here, choose that file and press "Import to this device".
  3. When it's done, open the slides and check that your practice records are there.
