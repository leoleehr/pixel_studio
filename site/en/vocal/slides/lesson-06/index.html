How to breathe when there's only a half-beat gap mid-line

Singing "把一個人的溫暖，轉移到另一個人的胸膛" from《愛情轉移》, the comma leaves only a half-beat gap, and the next line is long.

Which kind of breath works best here?

  1. A full breath, filling right up
  2. A catch breath (sneaked), a quick top-up
  3. Don't breathe, just push through to the end of the line
  4. Breathe between 溫 and 暖
A half-beat gap is too short for a full breath, so top up quickly with a catch breath and save full breaths for between phrases. Pushing through leaves the line end weak, and breathing in the middle of a word cuts it in two.

BuildUsable range vs. full range

What to go by when choosing songs and keys

Usable range

The range where your tone is steady, you can sing it again and again, and your throat stays relaxed.

Full range

From the lowest to the highest note you can make, including falsetto and notes you only just reach.

Which should you go by when choosing songs and keys?

  1. Full range
  2. Usable range
  3. The original singer's key
  4. The male and female keys on the song sheet
Go by your usable range. The full range is only a reference: notes you can only just reach will wear you out over a whole song. The male and female keys on song sheets are just average suggestions.
Build2. Counting intervals

Count the white keys, including both ends

On a keyboard, the number of white keys from your lowest note to your highest is the size of your range in degrees. C3 to C4 is 8 degrees (an octave).

Black keys aren't counted; sharps and flats count as the white key they belong to.

How many degrees from C3 to A4?

Counting the white keys from C3 to A4, including both ends, how many degrees is it?

  1. 12 degrees
  2. 13 degrees
  3. 10 degrees
  4. 21 degrees
C3 to C4 is 8 degrees, and above C4 the D, E, F, G, A add 5 more white keys: 8 + 5 = 13 degrees. The range of《泡沫》happens to be 13 degrees too.

Build3. Setting your own key

Let your highest usable note set the song's highest note

Semitones to transpose ＝ my highest usable note − the song's highest note

The song's range should be no bigger than your usable range. Make them equal, or drop the song another one or two semitones to leave some headroom.

Original key C, highest note A4; my highest usable note is F4. Which key should I use?

  1. A♭
  2. E
  3. F
  4. D
F4 − A4 = −4 semitones, and C down 4 semitones is A♭. On guitar, use G shapes with the capo at the 1st fret.
